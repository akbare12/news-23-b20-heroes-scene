# The World Today — Monday 23 November 2026

One image that sums up the world's news of Monday 23 November 2026:
three large hero scenes (the AI rout in Asia, Guterres on AI rules, the
US lead over China at a record low), ten illustrated story cards, a
numbered world map, and a DATE CHECK that keeps every story honest.

## Stories

31 stories across 6 regions. Heroes: KOSPI -3.3% (closes 4,708.54),
Guterres urges global AI safety rules at Bridgetown, Asia Power Index
2026 — US 78.9, China 73.6, gap 5.3. Everything dated 23 November 2026;
items that are not (Democrats 258, Sparta II, El Chapo, EU–Indonesia,
Nigeria boat, ICJ Myanmar) are listed in DATE CHECK as LEFT OUT, never
as stories.

## How it runs

`draw.py` draws `entry.png` (1024×1024, PIL). `main.py` copies it to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` and verifies size, byte count and sha256.
Scenes are drawn illustrations, not photographs.
